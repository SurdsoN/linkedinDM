    Install the update

    Chrome only updates Web Store extensions on its own. Because DM Architect is loaded from a folder, it updates by writing the new files into that folder. You choose the folder once, and Chrome asks you to allow it.

    Step 2 — Install

    Prefer to update by hand?

    1. Download the new zip and unzip it.
    2. Copy its contents over your current DM Architect folder (replace the files).
    3. Open chrome://extensions and click ↻ on DM Architect, or click .

    Your settings, keys, synced profile and saved drafts are kept: they live in Chrome's storage, not in the folder.
